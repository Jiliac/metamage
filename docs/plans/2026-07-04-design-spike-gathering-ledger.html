    Rules of use

    • Gold = structure. Frames, eyebrows, active states. Never encodes a value.
    • Rose↔teal = polarity. Matchups, deltas, above/below 50%. Neutral gray at the midpoint.
    • Pips, numbers & art = identity. An archetype keeps its rank number across tiles, table, scatter, and matrix; its art is its signature card.
    • Cards are round, chrome is cut. Only card-like objects (deck art tiles, thumbs) get rounded corners; panels and frames are notched or sharp.
    • Confidence = ink. Sample size drives fill opacity; under five matches, no claim is made at all.
    • Numbers are tabular, right-aligned, monospaced. Prose stays under 65 characters per line.
    • Art source: Scryfall art crops of each deck's signature card (embedded here for the spike; served proper with attribution in production).
